What is on it

  • The stage, its number, and one line on what it is for. Meera reads it in a glance, because the prospect is talking.
  • Three things the prospect tends to say at this stage. These come from reading past calls, not from guessing.
  • A tap for what happened. Four taps, one of them already pressed. The fewer words Meera types during the call, the more she hears.
  • One box for the prospect's own words. It is the only free text on the screen.
  • The next stage, named, and a way to end the call from any stage, because most calls end early.
